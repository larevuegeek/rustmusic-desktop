PRIVACY

RustMusic keeps your library, playlists, history and settings on this
computer. It has no analytics, no telemetry and no advertising, and never
uploads your music files.

It contacts other systems only in these cases:

- Update check at startup (rustmusic.dev): RustMusic version, operating
  system and processor architecture.
  Turn off: Settings > General > Check for updates.
- Artist portraits for your library (Deezer, Wikipedia): artist names.
  Turn off: Settings > Network & DLNA > Artist portrait downloads.
- On your request only: synced lyrics (LRCLIB), tag and cover lookups
  (MusicBrainz, Cover Art Archive, Deezer, iTunes), podcasts and internet
  radio, scrobbling once you connect an account (Last.fm, ListenBrainz),
  your own media servers, and devices of your local network.

Full privacy policy:
https://github.com/larevuegeek/rustmusic-desktop/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
